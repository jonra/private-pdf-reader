# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

Private PDF Reader does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal
data.

- **Your documents stay on your device.** PDFs are fetched directly from the
  address you opened (or read from your disk) and rendered locally in your
  browser. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **No remote code.** All libraries (PDF.js, pdf-lib) and fonts are bundled with
  the extension.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The extension makes no network requests other
  than fetching the PDF you asked to open.
- **Local storage.** The only thing the extension stores is your theme choice,
  kept in your browser's local storage.
- **Signatures.** Signatures you draw or type exist only in the open tab and in
  the signed PDF you choose to download.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/jonra/private-pdf-reader/issues>.
